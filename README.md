# Sales Analysis Dashboard

## Project Objective

This project analyzes the 2022 sales performance of Vrinda Store using Microsoft Excel. The goal is to identify customer purchasing behavior, sales trends, top-performing states, sales channels, and provide actionable business recommendations.

## Dataset Used 
- <a href="https://github.com/dheerajgoyal5375/Sales-Performance-Dashboard/blob/main/Sales%20Analysis%20dashboard%20(1).xlsx">Dataset</a>
 
## Questions Solved

1.Compare Sales vs Orders on a single chart.

2.Identify the highest sales and order month.

3.Analyze purchasing behavior of Men vs Women.

4.Evaluate different Order Statuses (Delivered, Cancelled, Returned, Refunded).

5.Identify the Top 10 States contributing to sales.

6.Analyze the relationship between Age Group and Gender based on orders.

7.Determine the highest-performing sales channels.

8.Identify the best-selling product categories.

9.Monitor key business metrics using interactive filters and slicers.

## Process

1.Collected and understood the sales dataset.

2.Performed data cleaning by handling missing values, correcting inconsistencies, and formatting data.

3.Created additional calculated columns where required.

5.Designed Pivot Charts for visual analysis.

6.Added interactive Slicers for dynamic filtering.

## Project Insights

1.Women contributed approximately 64–65% of total purchases, making them the primary customer segment.

2.Customers aged 30–49 years generated nearly 50% of overall sales.

3.Maharashtra, Karnataka, and Uttar Pradesh were the top three revenue-generating states.

4.Amazon, Flipkart, and Myntra accounted for the majority of online sales.

5.More than 90% of orders were successfully delivered, indicating strong order fulfillment.

6.Adult women shopping through leading e-commerce platforms represented the most valuable customer segment.

## Business Recommendation

Target women aged 30–49 years in Maharashtra, Karnataka, and Uttar Pradesh through promotional campaigns and offers on Amazon, Flipkart, and Myntra to improve sales.

## Final Conclusion

Based on the analysis, Vrinda Store should focus its marketing efforts on women aged 30–49 years, particularly in Maharashtra, Karnataka, and Uttar Pradesh. Promoting products through major e-commerce platforms such as Amazon, Flipkart, and Myntra using targeted advertisements, personalized offers, and discount campaigns can help increase customer engagement and drive higher sales.
